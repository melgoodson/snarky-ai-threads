Featured Design Scheduler — Admin Walkthrough

Date: July 23, 2026  |  Status: VERIFIED & RESOLVED
What's new: You can now schedule monthly featured design collections directly from the Admin Dashboard — no code changes needed. Upload images, auto-name them, pick which designs to spotlight, and preview exactly how the homepage will look.

How to Access

  1. Log in at /admin
  2. Click the Featured tab in the dashboard
Featured tab with month selector, headline fields, and upload zone

You'll see four main areas:

  • Month Selector (top-right) — pick any month to configure
  • Monthly Headline card — set the title and description
  • Upload New Designs — drag & drop images, they auto-name from filenames
  • Select Featured Designs — pick which designs to spotlight

Step 1: Pick Your Designs

Scroll down to the Select Featured Designs grid. Click any design thumbnail to select it — selected ones get an orange border and checkmark. The counter badge updates automatically.

Design picker grid with selectable thumbnails
💡 TIP: To upload new designs, use the Upload zone above the picker. Filenames auto-convert to titles — for example, snarky_coffee_cat.png becomes Snarky Coffee Cat.

Step 2: Set Headline & Subheadline

Fill in the Headline (shows as the big title on the homepage) and Subheadline (the description below it). Keep the Active checkbox checked so it goes live when the month arrives.

Filled form with headline, subheadline, and selected designs

Step 3: Preview

Click the Preview button (top-right, next to Save) to see exactly how the featured section will look on the homepage.

Live preview of the homepage featured section

The preview shows:

  • Your headline and subheadline
  • Theme badges (if you added any)
  • The selected designs in a grid with "FEATURED" badges

Step 4: Save

Click Save — you'll see a success confirmation. The schedule is now stored and will automatically display on the homepage when that month arrives.

Save confirmation notification

Quick Reference

Action How
Switch month Use the month dropdown (top-right)
Upload new designs Drag images into the Upload zone
Select/deselect a design Click its thumbnail
Add SEO theme badges Use the Theme Badges section (label + keywords)
Preview homepage look Click the Preview button
Save changes Click Save
Deactivate a month Uncheck the "Active" checkbox
📌 NOTE: If no schedule is saved for a month, the homepage automatically falls back to the default built-in themes — nothing breaks.
